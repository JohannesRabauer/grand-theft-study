# Three characters and local two-player co-op

## Description

A child can pick Lena, Mehmet or Jonas, and two players can share the town on one PC. The spec owns the capabilities; the game architecture spine owns the technical rules.

## Outcome

A child can pick Lena, Mehmet or Jonas, and two players can share the town on one PC.

## Done when

1. All three characters differ measurably in attributes, start vehicle and perks, and attributes grow with play.
2. Two players complete the commute and a chase together with one shared camera and tether.
3. Single-player and the prototype smoke test still pass.

## Boundaries

One capability area of the game, following the spec. Not other epics' areas; see the spec's non-goals.
