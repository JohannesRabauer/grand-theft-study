# In-world learning, chores and missions

## Description

Learning and chores happen in the town and at home, and side missions give reasons to explore. The spec owns the capabilities; the game architecture spine owns the technical rules.

## Outcome

Learning and chores happen in the town and at home, and side missions give reasons to explore.

## Done when

1. The quest runner drives chores and missions from data and shows objectives.
2. Pfand, bakery, signs, newspaper route and the tram map quiz work and feed the learning engine.
3. At least five chores and five missions are playable with rewards.

## Boundaries

One capability area of the game, following the spec. Not other epics' areas; see the spec's non-goals.
