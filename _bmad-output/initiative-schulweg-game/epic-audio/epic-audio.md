# Chiptune music and sound effects

## Description

The game sounds alive, and chases sound exciting. The spec owns the capabilities; the game architecture spine owns the technical rules.

## Outcome

The game sounds alive, and chases sound exciting.

## Done when

1. Music changes with phase and season and the chase layers rise with Ärger.
2. The listed sound effects exist.
3. Mobile starts muted until the first tap.

## Boundaries

One capability area of the game, following the spec. Not other epics' areas; see the spec's non-goals.
