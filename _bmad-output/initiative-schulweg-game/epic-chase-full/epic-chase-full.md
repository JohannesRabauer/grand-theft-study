# Full chase system: levels 3–5, boss and other chasers

## Description

Chases escalate through five levels with a boss, other chasers exist, and bullies can be talked or quizzed away. The spec owns the capabilities; the game architecture spine owns the technical rules.

## Outcome

Chases escalate through five levels with a boss, other chasers exist, and bullies can be talked or quizzed away.

## Done when

1. Levels 3–5 behave as specified and the boss trait changes play.
2. The Hausmeister, inspector, neighbor dog and angry neighbor trigger from their causes and each has a way out.
3. Charme and learning-question back-offs work at levels 1–2.

## Boundaries

One capability area of the game, following the spec. Not other epics' areas; see the spec's non-goals.
